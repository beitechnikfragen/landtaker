# Landtaker Release Notes

## 0.2.0 — Say something

Talking to the people you are playing with, during the match rather than
around it.

**In-game chat**

- Free-text chat while you play: **All**, **Team** (teammates and current
  allies), or a private whisper — alongside the existing quick phrases
- One tab per conversation, each with its own unread badge and its own
  half-typed draft, so answering a whisper never eats the sentence you
  were writing
- **Enter** opens the composer, **Tab** switches conversation, **Esc**
  closes it. Game hotkeys stay quiet while you type
- 200 characters per message and a short cooldown, so nobody can flood
  the log

**Social**

- The friends and party dock no longer vanishes when a match starts — it
  moves to the bottom left, clear of the chat, and starts collapsed so it
  never covers the map on entry
- Party members show real online status. The roster dot was previously
  always green, whether or not anyone was actually there

**Interface**

- The radial menu now matches the rest of the HUD: dark plates with
  hairline seams and a single accent, instead of six colour washes
  competing with the map underneath
- Shared links carry our own preview card, title and icon
- Installing the game to a home screen no longer shows the old icon

## 0.1.0 — First light

Landtaker begins. This build carries the new identity end to end and the
first pieces of our own infrastructure.

**Identity & Home**

- New name, mark and military design language across the whole app — home,
  subpages, modals and the in-game HUD
- The play screen is built around what's live: deployments with map
  previews, open lobbies, and your own standing
- Commander rail with rank insignia, division progress, record and match
  history

**Ranked & Accounts**

- Own backend: accounts, ranked rating, win/loss record and match archive
- Rank tiers Bronze to Platinum, three divisions each — thresholds are
  provisional while the ladder calibrates

**Social**

- Friends with realtime 1:1 chat and online presence
- Parties live in the social dock: invite code, roster, party chat
- Free-for-all refuses parties — FFA is solo by definition
